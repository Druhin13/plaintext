The quarterly review of our distribution strategy has reached a point where the board expects a firm recommendation rather than a further round of analysis. Revenue in the northern region grew by eleven percent against a forecast of six, and that outperformance has been concentrated almost entirely in the two accounts we acquired last autumn. Stripping those out, underlying growth is closer to two percent, which is below the rate of inflation in the markets where we operate. The finance team has flagged this repeatedly and I think we should stop presenting the headline number without the adjustment.

Our margin position is more comfortable than the growth figure suggests. Gross margin improved by ninety basis points, driven by a renegotiated logistics contract and by the decision to discontinue the three lowest-volume product lines. Neither of those levers is repeatable. The logistics contract runs until the end of next year and the counterparty has already indicated that renewal terms will be less favourable. We should assume a reversion of at least half the improvement.

The competitive picture has changed in ways that were not obvious twelve months ago. Two regional competitors have merged and the combined entity now has enough scale to bid on the national contracts we have historically won without serious opposition. Their pricing in the first three tenders since the merger has been aggressive to the point of being uneconomic, which suggests they are buying share deliberately. We can either match them and accept a period of depressed margin, or concede those contracts and concentrate on the specialist segments where our service model is genuinely differentiated.

My recommendation is the second course, with one qualification. Conceding the national contracts entirely would remove a volume base that our manufacturing footprint currently depends on. Before we make that decision we need a clear view of what happens to unit cost if volumes fall by a quarter. The operations team has been asked to model this and should report before the end of the month.
