=============================================================================

-------------------  -----------------  ---------------
0.05                 78                 54.6

Glyphmark's published anchor table gives region-change rates of 0.21 at
40-char windows / 20% deletion and 0.51 at 100-char windows / 20% deletion.
Both are already out of reach, so validation bits are mandatory, not optional.
